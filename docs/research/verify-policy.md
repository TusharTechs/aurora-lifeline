# Fact-check: policy and disaster claims

Adversarial fact-check run on 27-28 Sep 2026. Verdicts: CONFIRMED, PARTLY (detail corrected), REFUTED, UNVERIFIED.

| P1 | IMD/NDMA's cyclone Web-DCRA gives village/tehsil/district composite loss estimates from IMD forecasts, built under NCRMP; operational in 2026; computes road-network access loss to hospitals/shelters or power-outage cascades. | PARTLY | Confirmed: NDMA built the Web-DCRA & DSS tool under NCRMP. It overlays IMD cyclone forecasts (wind, surge, inland flooding) on exposure data. NDMA launched it in Sep 2022 and used it in Biparjoy and Michaung (2023). Not confirmed: that it runs in 2026 (no 2026 source found, and ncrmp.gov.in timed out); that it works at village or tehsil level (press describes district-specific warnings); and that it does road-network access analysis to hospitals/shelters or power-outage cascades (no source describes either). The World Bank also noted in Jan 2025 that the project-funded Web-DCRA mobile app was never released. | https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099021825083513126/pdf/P144726-bfd1dbca-dfec-4100-893b-09d38239f293.pdf | Also opened: the MHA Rajya Sabha reply of 2 Apr 2025 (mha.gov.in RS02042025/3600.pdf: used in Biparjoy and Michaung); an IMD RSMC 2024 training agenda with a session on impact-based TC forecasting using Web DCRA (rsmcnewdelhi.imd.gov.in/uploads/home/conference/rsmc_training2024.pdf); and thehawk.in (district-specific warnings covering roads and power lines as damage categories, not network analysis). The network-analysis gap looks like real room for AURORA to add something, but it is based on missing evidence, not proven. |
| P2 | INCOIS runs an operational ADCIRC-based storm-surge and coastal-inundation forecast driven by IMD tracks (since Cyclone Hudhud 2014). | CONFIRMED |  | https://tsunami.incois.gov.in/TEWS/AboutStormSurge.jsp | The INCOIS page names ADCIRC as its storm-surge prediction model. INCOIS's Vardah bulletin (tsunami.incois.gov.in/itews/ssm/Vardah_B8.html) shows surge and inundation extent 'based on latest forecast issued by IMD'. A 2023 INCOIS presentation hosted by NRSC (nrsc.gov.in DRM_Meet_2023) says the Storm Surge Early Warning System has issued real-time warnings for about 55 cyclones. The 'first real-time use during Hudhud (Oct 2014), after an experimental run for Phailin (2013)' detail comes only from search snippets of the Ocean Engineering paper; the paywalled page returned 403. |
| P3 | Cyclone Montha (Oct 2025) destroyed about 13,000 power lines, 3,000 transformers and 14 bridges in India, and caused 8 deaths in India. | PARTLY | It was about 13,000 electric POLES damaged, not power lines, plus about 3,000 km of conductor lines and about 3,000 transformers. The 14 figure is 'bridges and culverts'. All of these are Andhra Pradesh figures only, and 'damaged' is more accurate than 'destroyed'. Deaths: the AP Chief Minister said 2 in AP and Telangana reported at least 6, so about 8 in India, but early reports said 3 in AP. | https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/andhra-pradesh/cyclone-montha-damages-13000-power-poles-3000-transformers-in-ap-1913863 | Also opened: etvbharat.com ('14 bridges and culverts', 2 deaths per the AP CM); telanganatoday.com (at least six dead in Telangana); en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclone_Montha (8 direct deaths; landfall near Narasapuram on 28-29 Oct 2025). Wikipedia's 'power lines' wording is wrong. |
| P4 | Deep depression 'BOB 06' crossed the north Andhra Pradesh coast near Kalingapatnam around 21-27 Sep 2026 and caused about 88 deaths, 56 of them inland in Uttar Pradesh. | PARTLY | 21-27 Sep is the system's lifetime. It crossed the coast on the night of 23 Sep 2026 (about 2300 IST on 23 Sep to 0100 IST on 24 Sep), just southwest of Kalingapatnam, as a Deep Depression. The 88 total comes from Wikipedia's aggregate and INCLUDES about 14 deaths in Nepal. India alone is about 74 (UP 56, AP 9, Chhattisgarh 7, Odisha 2). Press counts differ: Al Jazeera gives 56 in UP plus 12 in Nepal (68). The UP figure of 56 is confirmed and was attributed by the UP Relief Commissioner to the Bay of Bengal depression moving inland. | https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/27/floods-and-landslides-kill-at-least-56-people-in-india-12-in-nepal | Also opened: IMD RSMC Special Tropical Weather Outlook of 23.09.2026 (rsmcnewdelhi.imd.gov.in ... 23.09.2026.pdf: Deep Depression 30 km SSW of Kalingapatnam, crossing 1500-1800 UTC 23 Sep); odishabytes.com (IMD: crossed near Kalingapatnam about 11 pm 23 Sep); sea.weathernews.com/resources/202609/246 (designation BOB/06/2026); freemalaysiatoday.com (UP Relief Commissioner, 56 dead); en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_North_Indian_Ocean_cyclone_season (88 total with state breakdown). Tolls may still rise. |

| P9 | A parametric cyclone insurance pilot 'Cyclone-Ready' (SEEDS, Howden India, New India Assurance) covering ~2,500 households in Cuddalore launched in Oct 2025; Nagaland has a parametric disaster cover with SBI General. | CONFIRMED |  | https://pnndigital.com/national/cyclone-ready-piloting-indias-first-of-its-kind-parametric-insurance-for-coastal-communities/ | Syndicated press release titled 'Cyclone-Ready': SEEDS, Howden India and New India Assurance (with a local NGO partner), Cuddalore district in Tamil Nadu, about 2,500 families (another report says 2,537), launched 29 Oct 2025. | P10 | India has ~32 crore people vulnerable to cyclones and a 7,516 km coastline (NDMA). | PARTLY | Both figures come from the NDMA National Guidelines on Management of Cyclones dated 24 April 2008: 7,516 km coastline, about 32 crore people (about one-third of the population) vulnerable. They are out of date. India's official coastline was re-assessed to 11,098.81 km (MoPSW circular of 29 Apr 2025; confirmed in a MoES parliament reply, PIB 4 Dec 2025), and NDMA's current cyclone web page gives 8,041 km. The 32 crore estimate predates current population figures. Cite it as 'NDMA 2008 estimate' and use 11,098.81 km for the coastline. | https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2198800 | Also opened: nidm.gov.in/PDF/pubs/NDMA/4.pdf (NDMA Cyclone Guidelines 2008, giving 7,516 km and 32 crore) and ndma.gov.in/Natural-Hazards/Cyclone (8,041 km; no population figure). |
